# The irrationality exponent of <i>π</i> is 2

The following describes the scope of the Lean formalization related to the following accompanying paper(s):

- [The irrationality exponent of $\pi$ is 2](../../preprints/The-irrationality-exponent-of-pi-is-2-September-24-2026/paper.pdf)

## Scope

The formalization proves that the irrationality exponent of $\pi$ is exactly two. For every $\nu>2$, all sufficiently large positive denominators $q$ satisfy $|\pi-p/q|\ge q^{-\nu}$ for every integer numerator $p$. It also states the exact supremum characterization using infinitely many rational approximations.

The paper's convergence consequence for the classical Flint–Hills series (Corollary 1.2) is outside the selected Comparator statement in `ComparatorChallenges/PiExponent.lean`, though it is formalized and proved in `OAI/NumberTheory/PiExponent/Main.lean` (`flint_hills_summable`); the two-parameter generalized series (Corollary 5.2) is outside the formalization.
